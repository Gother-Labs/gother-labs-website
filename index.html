01 / Fit

When a challenge is useful

You already have an optimizer. The open question is whether it leaves value on the table.

01

A baseline exists

There is a policy, heuristic, solver, or dispatch rule with behavior your team can replay.

02

The rules can be frozen

Constraints, scenarios, scoring, and acceptance checks can be agreed before the search begins.

03

The decision matters

An improvement candidate is valuable only if engineers can inspect it, replay it, and challenge it.

02 / Method

A governed offline loop

Freeze the test before searching for the answer.

The evaluation contract is the boundary. Candidates can change; the scoring rules, constraints, and required evidence do not.

  1. 01

    Brief

    Start with business context and non-confidential metadata. No fixture upload is requested at first contact.

  2. 02

    Freeze

    Agree the baseline, evaluation fixture, constraints, score, replay checks, and publication boundary.

  3. 03

    Challenge

    Search offline for candidates and reject anything that fails the frozen contract.

  4. 04

    Evidence

    Return the accepted candidate with score traces, weak cases, replay artifacts, and limitations.

What this is—and is not

It is

An offline comparison against an agreed baseline, under a frozen evaluation contract.

It returns

Candidate improvements and the evidence required to reproduce, inspect, or reject them.

It is not

A production deployment, live-trading result, hidden integration, or automatic replacement for your optimizer.
