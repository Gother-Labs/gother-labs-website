01 / Thesis

Better should be measurable. A promising result should survive scrutiny.

Important algorithms should not improve through intuition alone. Göther works on bounded technical surfaces where the baseline is visible, the rules can be frozen, and a candidate has to earn the right to move forward.

03 / Discipline

Freeze the test before searching for the answer.

The evaluation contract is the boundary. Candidates can change; the rules used to judge them do not.

  1. 01

    Freeze

    Agree the baseline, fixture, constraints, score, replay checks, and evidence boundary.

  2. 02

    Challenge

    Search offline for candidates without changing the test used to compare them.

  3. 03

    Replay

    Run baseline and candidate through the same fixture and inspect weak cases.

  4. 04

    Decide

    Accept only what satisfies the frozen contract; document rejection and limitations.
