Company / A note from the founder

An independent lab.
A clear standard.

There is often room for a better implementation. The interesting question is whether we can find it—and show that it holds up.

Göther Labs explores that question in technical systems where small improvements can matter. We start with something that works, search for alternatives, and measure them against rules agreed in advance.

A specific place to begin.

Our current commercial focus is RTL/PPA optimization: one owned RTL block, a defined implementation flow, and an outcome an engineering team can inspect. Our public research explores the same discipline across scientific computing, scheduling, geometry, quantum compilation and energy.

Small, by design. Accountable, in practice.

I run Göther as its sole permanent member, supported by AI agents for research, implementation and testing. I remain responsible for the scope of the work, its technical claims and its delivery. A client's engineering team decides whether a result is suitable for adoption.

The limits belong with the result.

A stronger aggregate number is only part of the story. The baseline, constraints, weaker cases and limitations matter too. A documented no-improvement finding is a valid outcome.

The ambition is to build a lab whose work earns confidence through what others can inspect.

Juan José Fernández Morales

Founder, Göther Labs · September 2026
