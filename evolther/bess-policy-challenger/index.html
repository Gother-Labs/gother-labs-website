Private challenge format.

The first engagement is deliberately small. We freeze the contract before searching, then return only candidates that replay cleanly under the agreed rules.

01

What you send

Battery spec, approved scenarios, current policy fixture, constraint rules, target metrics, and known limitations.

02

What we freeze

Evaluation contract, baseline replay, scenario splits, scoring function, guardrails, and accepted-artifact requirements.

03

What we return

Improvement candidates, comparison rows, dispatch traces, replay checks, downside readout, and a decision memo.

04

What we do not claim

No live bidding claim, no market-access claim, no replacement of your optimizer, and no hidden production integration.
